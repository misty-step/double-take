            How to play

            Everyone gets the same two worlds. Write the line that fits both best.

            Same words. Tap to see the other world.

            • Each world rates your line from 0 to 3. Your points are both added together. Six is a double take.
            • One sentence, twelve words at most. Stitched halves, safe lines, and lines one world rejects score nothing.
            • Lines are revealed without names. Most points takes the round. Three rounds.
