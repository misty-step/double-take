# Double Take design and copy

The group game gives everyone the same two worlds and asks for one line that fits both. White pages hold the room, writing, and results; each world's own color fills the writing ends and the timed reveal. The line stays in place while the second world wipes over the first. Newsreader gives the words room to perform; Libre Franklin keeps actions and scores direct.

The mark is a blush and navy rounded square divided by a white seam. The 16 px icon is optically simplified for tabs. Actions use plain names: Start a game, Join a game, Lock it in, Next round, See who won, Play again. Errors explain a safe next action without exposing internal details.

The authoritative screens, motion, copy, and accessibility specification is [design/reimagine/DESIGN.md](../design/reimagine/DESIGN.md); the interactive reference is `design/reimagine/synthesis/index.html`.
